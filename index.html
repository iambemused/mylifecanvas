📲 How to Sync

1
Capture your tasks using the quick buttons. They're saved on this device until you export them.
2
Tap "Export Captures" when you're ready. This creates a small file with all your captures.
3
Send the file to yourself — email it, save to Dropbox/Google Drive, AirDrop to your computer, or any way you prefer.
4
On your desktop, open MyLifeCanvas → Settings → "Import Captures". Select the file, and your captures become real tasks.

Why this way? MyLifeCanvas keeps your data private — no accounts, no cloud sync we control. You choose how to move your data between devices.

💡 Tip: Add this to your home screen for quick access! In your browser, tap Share (iOS) or Menu (Android) → "Add to Home Screen".
